🟢 Sunday — Arrival + First Adventures

12:00 PM — Arrive at the Park

📍 14421 346th St, Louisville, NE 68037
🚗 Check in, drive to campsite and park next to the tent area.
💡 Bathrooms, showers, picnic table and fire pit available.

3:30 PM — Waterfall Glen + Suspension Bridge

🚗 About 5 minutes from most campsites.
🚶 Easy short walk.
Beautiful creek, waterfall and bridge views.

6:45 PM — Jenny Newman Memorial Tower

🌅 Best sunset view in the park.
🚗 5–10 minutes from the campground.

8:00 PM — Campfire Night 🔥

Hot chocolate + marshmallows at the campsite.
Cozy and perfect for a relaxing night under the stars ✨

🔵 Monday — Nature Day

9:00 AM — Breakfast at the Campsite

10:30 AM — Stone Creek Falls

🚗 About 5–10 minutes driving inside the park.
🚶 Easy/moderate walk through forest and creeks.

1:30 PM — Cheap Student-Friendly Lunch 🍕

📍 Casey’s — 1300 Silver St, Ashland, NE 68003
🚗 10–12 minutes away.

📍 Runza — 1018 County Rd 66, Ashland, NE 68003
🚗 10–15 minutes away.

4:00 PM — Relax at the Campsite

9:00 PM — Stargazing ✨

Best directly from the campsite.
Bring a blanket, chair and hot chocolate ☕

🟡 Tuesday — Easy Morning + Departure

9:30 AM — Last Short Walk

Suggested: revisit Waterfall Glen in the peaceful morning light 🌊

11:00 AM — Pack Up the Campsite

⏰ Usually takes 1–2 hours.

2:00 PM — Departure 🚗
